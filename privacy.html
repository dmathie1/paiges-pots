Privacy

This site is run by Paige, a hobby ceramicist. It only collects the details you choose to send, and only uses them for the reason you sent them.

What I collect

  • Sign-up for new pieces: your email address.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, the piece you are asking about and your message.
  • Buying a piece: your name, email address and delivery address, collected at checkout.

The site does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

How I use it

  • Your sign-up email is only used to tell you about new pieces.
  • Contact form details are only used to reply to you and, if you buy or commission something, to arrange it.

I never sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing.

Services the site uses

  • Formspree receives the contact and sign-up forms and passes them on to me by email.
  • Stripe handles checkout when you buy a piece. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never seen or stored by me; I receive your name, email and delivery address so I can post your order.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Instagram links open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

How long I keep it

Sign-up emails are kept until you ask to be removed. Messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or order.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details I hold about you, have them corrected, or be removed at any time. Just get in touch or message me on Instagram. If you are unhappy with how your details have been handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
