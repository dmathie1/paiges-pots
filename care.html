Care

Looking after your piece

  • Hand wash with warm water and mild soap. It is gentler on glazes and hand-painted details than a dishwasher.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes. Do not move a piece from the fridge or freezer into a hot oven, or pour boiling water into a cold cup.
  • Check the piece's page first. Unless a piece is listed as microwave safe, please heat food and drinks another way.
  • Sculptures and boxes are decorative. Pieces such as the cathedral, card boxes and cigarette box are not made for food or drink.
  • Handle with care. Ceramics can chip or crack if dropped or knocked against hard surfaces.

Every piece is one of a kind

Each piece is made and painted by hand, so small differences in size, shape, colour and glaze are normal and are part of what makes it handmade. I photograph pieces as accurately as I can, but colours can look slightly different on your screen.

Questions?

Not sure how to look after a particular piece? Get in touch and I will be happy to help.
